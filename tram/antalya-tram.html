    Live where it can be. Every Antalya route is here — 6 tram lines and 163 bus routes, all from the same feed. Kentkart's passenger API reports the vehicles' own GPS, and a live row is a tram the feed can actually see, its arrival worked out from the stop it last called at — which only works once it is on the road behind you — so when nothing is on your side of the line, the note above the board says what is coming the other way and how far off it is. A timed row is the timetable — the current one, fetched with the positions. With no signal the page falls back to a timetable baked in from a 2023 export and says so above — that export covers the trams only, so a bus with no signal has nothing to show.
