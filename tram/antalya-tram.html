    Where the timetable puts them

    Positions worked out from the schedule — not where the trams actually are.

    Scheduled times, not live positions. Antalya publishes no public real-time feed for the trams, so this is the operator's own timetable counted down against the clock. A tram running late will not show as late here — and the newest public copy of that timetable was exported in 2023, so read it as the pattern of service rather than gospel.
